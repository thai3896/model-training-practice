# Voice Editor & AI Voice Clone Demo Script

### 🎤 Part 1: Your Real Voice (The Intro)
*(Action: You are on camera or showing the main screen, speaking naturally).*

"Hey everyone, welcome to a quick demo of what we've been building. Today, I want to show you two completely different pieces of AI tech working together. 

First, I'm going to show you a powerful AI dictation tool we built called **Voice Editor**. 
Second, I want to show off the power of open-source Voice Cloning. In fact, to prove just how good the cloning is, I'm going to stop talking, and let my AI clone take over right now to walk you through the app."

---

### 🤖 Part 2: The AI Clone (The Product Demo)
*(Action: You play the synthesized AI audio file while screen-recording yourself using the `voice-editor` app).*

"Thanks for the handoff. Yes, this is my AI clone speaking! Let's take a look at the Voice Editor. 

This tool is designed to be your ultimate AI writing assistant. You simply click the microphone in the corner and start speaking. As you dictate, our backend runs Whisper to transcribe your words almost instantly—and it perfectly supports both English and Vietnamese.

If you look at the bottom of the screen, you'll see the **'AI Cleanup'** toggle. When this is enabled, the editor acts like a live proofreader, automatically removing stutters, fixing grammar, and formatting your sentences on the fly while you speak.

But the real magic happens when you highlight text. Our AI Bubble Menu pops up, allowing you to instantly 'Improve writing', 'Proofread', or 'Reformat' your thoughts. You can even type a custom prompt to ask the AI to rewrite a specific paragraph directly inside your document. 

It makes dictating and refining your work completely frictionless. And as you can hear... having an AI clone narrate your product demos for you isn't too bad either!"

---

### 🎤 Part 3: Your Real Voice (The Outro)
*(Action: You take over the microphone again).*

"And we're back! As you can see, combining a fluid AI text editor with custom AI voice generation completely changes how we create content. Thanks for watching, and let me know what you think!"

***

## 💡 How to produce this:
1. Copy the text from **Part 2** and use your voice clone to generate the `.wav` file.
2. Record a video of your screen clicking around the `voice-editor` app demonstrating the features mentioned.
3. In any basic video editor (like CapCut, Premiere, or iMovie), drop the AI `.wav` file over the screen recording.
4. Record your intro and outro audio on the ends!
